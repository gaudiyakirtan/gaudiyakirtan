Accuracy benchmark

R@k = share of attempts where the intended song ranked in the top k. MRR = mean reciprocal rank. p50/p95 = per-query latency over the ground-truth set. Many R@1 misses are ties between genuinely near-identical titles (śocaka (1)/(2)/(3)), which is why R@5 is the fairer headline.
